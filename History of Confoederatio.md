#history #confoederatio

Unlike most comparable studios, Confoederatio cannot pretend to have a long, noble history; but a rapid, tumultuous one, short on people and long on grandiose names, often borrowed from the vocabulary of mapgames. 

From 2021 onwards, much of the community was single-handedly driven forwards by Vis and Aust, rendering it more biography than the story of a community of any sort. At the same time, Confoederatio/Midnight's history is important in the sense that its mods and products have been downloaded hundreds of thousands of times, and viewed perhaps millions of times.

From an organisational level, sustained popularity proved elusive, and valuable attention could be said to have been squandered. It therefore forms also forms a repository of what not to do.
