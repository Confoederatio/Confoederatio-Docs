## Abstract

Current Version: **No Stable Version**

**Collation** is our OSINT suite for geospatialised news aggregation and tracking live events and ORBATs. Because its data pipelines are currently being consolidated, it requires local self-hosting and is mainly intended for developer use at its current stage. It also conveniently functions as the main dev branch for [Naissance](/Naissance).

Collation is based off ⁠Naissance GIS: Software/Map Edi… with composite sourcing from Liveuamap, Project Owl, USNI, AIS Data/GFW, social media filtering, and other live monitoring vendors. Automated georeferencing is based off [**Deprojector**](/Deprojector). 

Improvements to Collation are currently secondary priorities due to **Confoederatio**'s main focus on Histmap, and improvements are slated for sometime in 2027.
