**Naissance** is a full-spectrum GIS developed by [Confoederatio](https://confoederatio.org/) for editing historical and contemporary maps over time. Base data is provided by the Naissance World Model.  
The editor, and all corresponding data and projects are open-source and free to use with attribution.
